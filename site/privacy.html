Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Labelarium collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you save stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To remember your choices, Labelarium stores a few things in your browser's local storage on your own device:

  • label makers you pin, and the last one you opened
  • label designs you save in the label preview
  • which label makers you saved for offline use, plus their pictures in the app's offline cache
  • whether you dismissed the install prompt

None of this is sent anywhere. Clearing the app's site data, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Network requests

The app only downloads its own files (pages, pictures and label maker data) from labelarium.com. It makes no requests to third parties. Our host, GitHub Pages, may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Labelarium hands a link to your device's share sheet or copies it to your clipboard. You choose where it goes; Labelarium never sees it. A shared label design is encoded in the link itself.

Links to other sites

Links such as “Open source on GitHub”, “Buy me a coffee” and the author's profile open other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

Labelarium is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change too. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/jatinkrmalik/labelarium.
